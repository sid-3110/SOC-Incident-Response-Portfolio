## Incident Type:
Phishing Email

## Severity:
High

## Status:
Escalated

## Summary:
A phishing email impersonating internal IT support was delivered to a finance user and reported for investigation.

## Assessment:
The email failed SPF, DKIM, and DMARC checks and used urgency-based social engineering with a deceptive link. User interaction was confirmed, increasing the risk of credential compromise.

## Action Taken:
Incident identified and escalated to the Incident Response team for containment and remediation.
