## Incident Type:
Suspicious Authentication Activity

## Severity:
Medium

## Status:
Escalated

## Summary:
Multiple failed login attempts followed by a successful authentication were observed for a finance user account.

## Assessment:
The authentication pattern, source IP repetition, and off-hours activity indicate potential credential misuse.

## Action Taken:
Alert escalated to SOC Level 2 for validation and response.

## MITRE Mapping:
Credential Access
